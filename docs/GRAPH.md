### Sweet–Bitch / Patriarchal–Feminist Graph of Princesses

This scatter plot maps princess characters on two axes:

- **X-axis (Left → Right):** Patriarchal (0%) to Feminist (100%)  
  → Measures how much agency, independence, and progressive values the character shows.

- **Y-axis (Bottom → Top):** Sweet (0%) to Bitch (100%)  
  → Measures assertiveness, boldness, and attitude, ranging from gentle to fierce.

The graph visualizes the evolution of princess archetypes—from passive, romance-driven figures to independent, empowered heroines—across Disney and anime.
